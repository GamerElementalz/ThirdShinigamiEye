# ThirdShinigamiEye
Peek inside any chest in the Basement before opening it!

Say goodbye to wasting keys.

Mod idea by [ggNova](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9olSHkUPEr1pjenUy2it-Q) and [synthwave](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCn_pYDrdkqe87AEmzVVdBnA).

Mod name inspired by a similar game mechanic in Persona 5 and the Shinigami Eyes from Death Note.
